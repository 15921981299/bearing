## High-Intent Keyword Coverage Added

The model templates now create useful, readable sourcing descriptions around these patterns:

- `[model] combined bearing replacement`
- `[model] combined roller bearing dimensions`
- `[model] mast guide bearing`
- `WINKEL [model] compatible bearing`
- `[cross-reference] bearing equivalent`
- `[NbV profile] profile combined bearing`
- `[model] bearing supplier`

The WINKEL cross-reference guide now covers additional purchasing references used in current search results:

- W-series references such as `W-4.058`
- legacy `984.xxx` references
- PBC Hevi-Rail `HVB-xxx` and `HVBEA-xxx`
- MAX Rail `MX-xx` and `MX-4xx`
- existing MR, TR, JD, CR and 400-series references

These were added to existing model and cross-reference pages instead of creating another large batch of near-duplicate pages.

## Image Use Rules

1. Product-family images are configuration illustrations only; dimensions and execution come from the specification table and controlled drawing.
2. Documentary factory images may support manufacturing evidence. Their legacy overlays were removed, but the original scene was retained.
3. Generated application images may explain use cases but must not be presented as a customer installation, shipment, inspection record or factory certificate.
4. Technical drawings and order-specific inspection photos take precedence when available.

## Path Toward 30 Qualified Inquiries per Month

Thirty inquiries per month is a commercial target, not a ranking guarantee. Measure progress with qualified RFQs rather than raw form submissions.

| Funnel signal | Monthly operating target | Why it matters |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| High-intent organic sessions | 1,200–2,000 | Supports roughly 1.5–2.5% inquiry conversion scenarios |
| RFQ starts | 45–60 | Indicates product and CTA relevance |
| Completed inquiries | 30 | Primary target |
| Inquiries containing model or drawing | 18+ | Higher sales qualification |
| Core-model pages receiving impressions | 20–30 | Confirms demand concentration |

Review by landing page, country, model number, cross-reference, device, RFQ source and whether a drawing was supplied. Do not add more programmatic pages until Search Console shows which model and application clusters are gaining impressions but have weak click-through or conversion.
